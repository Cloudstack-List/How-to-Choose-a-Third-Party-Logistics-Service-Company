What a Third Party Logistics Service Company Actually Does

A third party logistics service company takes care of the storage, order processing, packing, and courier coordination that keep your business moving. Instead of running your own warehouse, a 3PL handles the heavy work so your items reach customers quickly and safely.

A typical 3PL covers:

  • Warehouse storage
  • Order picking and packing
  • Courier dispatch
  • Delivery tracking
  • Return handling
  • Inventory monitoring
  • Daily reporting

For e-commerce sellers on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, and Shopify, this support removes delays that often come from manual workflows.

Common Logistics Problems That Hold Businesses Back

Before choosing a 3PL, it helps to understand the challenges growing brands usually face.

Manual inventory updates

Updating spreadsheets creates errors and delays when marketplaces sync stock.

Delayed fulfillment

Packing orders late affects shipping cut-offs and your seller rating.

Courier coordination issues

Missed pick-ups lead to customer complaints and canceled orders.

Limited visibility

Without a dashboard, you can’t see your stock levels or outbound orders in real time.

High operating costs

Warehouse rent, manpower, and equipment can drain cash flow.

These are the pain points most 3PLs claim to fix, but not all of them offer a true system that connects every channel.
